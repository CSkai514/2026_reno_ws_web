Individual Care

Treatments

A treatment as individual as you. At Wong & Sim Dental, we put you first. Our dentists will be happy to discuss your needs in one of our comfortable, well-equipped clinics.

Patient-First Dentistry

Clinical Excellence
defined by your needs.

Whether you're visiting us for a preventative check-up or complex restorative surgery, our multidisciplinary team is here to listen to your story and provide care that aligns with your specific goals. Every treatment at Wong & Sim Dental is delivered with precision, compassion, and a commitment to your long-term oral health.

01

General Dentistry

General dentistry is the foundation of what we do. Whether you'd like a routine check up, are interested in cleaning services, fillings or need an emergency appointment, we can help.

02

Guided Biofilm Therapy with AirFlow

Guided Biofilm Therapy is for anyone looking for gentle and effective prophylaxis. This minimally invasive and more comfortable solution is suitable for both adults and children.

03

Teeth Whitening

If you are dreaming of a whiter smile, we can make it a reality. Home teeth whitening is a simple solution that can immediately improve the colour of your teeth and boost your self-confidence.

04

Orthodontic & Invisalign

We offer traditional braces, but we also have options for near-invisible teeth straightening solutions. No-one will know you are wearing braces, so you can keep smiling even through treatment.

05

Veneers

A beautiful smile does wonders to our confidence. Porcelain veneers are thin laminates placed over the front part of the tooth to recreate a youthful and natural-looking smile.

06

Gum Disease Treatment

Gum disease is silent, so it’s important to get a professional to check for signs regularly. As with any disease, if caught early it is much easier treat, with better results.

07

Root Canal Treatment

If you have been experiencing severe tooth pain, it is possible that you may require root canal treatment. We can treat your tooth without the need to remove it, leaving you pain-free and in good oral health.

08

Dental Implant

Implant is a fixed solution for one or more missing teeth, and can help restore both your smile and your quality of life.

09

Oral Surgery

From a simple dental extraction or wisdom tooth surgery to the complex oral surgery, we’re prepared to take care of your needs.

10

Children's Dentistry

Establishing a good routine of oral hygiene and dental care at a young age will help your child develop healthy milk teeth, and later on will establish a set of strong, straight permanent teeth.

11

Laser Therapy

We are able perform procedures using state of the art dental lasers to reduce discomfort, shorten treatment times, minimise complications and ensure a comfortable healing period.

12

Dental Crowns & Inlays/Onlays

If you have damage to a tooth that cannot be fixed with a filling. We can fit a crown, inlays or onlays to cover the damage and restore your tooth to its natural looking state.

13

Dental Bridge

Dental bridges are a popular and highly succesfull way of restoring your smile and function if you're missing one of your teeth. Living with missing teeth can affect your day-to-day confidence and your health.

14

Care & Advice

From how to deal with dental phobias, to advice on common dental conditions, how to keep teeth in top shape and your breath fresh, our team can help with all your queries on general dental care and advice.

15

Other Treatments

We can help with a whole host of oral concerns, from creating custom mouthguards, treating oral diseases, to providing smile solutions.
