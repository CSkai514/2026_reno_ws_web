Our Philosophy

Every smile tells a
unique story — and
we're here to listen.

Wong & Sim Dental Clinic is a trusted destination for exceptional dental care in Penang, with three established locations — Summerton in Bayan Lepas, Pulau Tikus in Georgetown and Bukit Mertajam.

We believe dentistry is about more than treating teeth. It is about caring for people. Guided by minimally invasive principles, we provide modern dental care defined by precision and compassion.
